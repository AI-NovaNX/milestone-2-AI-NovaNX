Game Instructions

  • This game requires at least a tablet-sized screen. It is not recommended for use on a phone-sized screen.
  • First, place all your ships on your board.
  • Hover over your board to preview ship placement.
  • Green preview means valid. Red preview means invalid.
  • Click enemy board to fire once the battle starts.
  • 💥 means hit, 🌊 means miss.
  • Hard mode gives the computer a much smarter attack pattern.
