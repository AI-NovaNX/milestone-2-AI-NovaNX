Avoid the Falling Objects

Avoid the falling objects for as long as possible to get the highest score.

Game Instructions

  • Enter your nickname before playing.
  • Use the ← and → keys to move.
  • Avoid all objects falling from above.
  • If your head is hit by any falling object other than 💎, it is game over immediately.
  • Every object you successfully avoid increases your score.
  • If you hit a 💎, you get a 50-point bonus and it is not game over.
  • Press Start Game to begin.
